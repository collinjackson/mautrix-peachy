# Connect Peachy on Beeper Desktop

[← Peachy for Beeper](../index.md)

For Beeper Desktop on Mac, Windows and Linux.

> **Early testing.** These steps work once someone has the bridge running
> (see [Run the bridge](run-the-bridge.md)).

## Before you start

- The bridge is running ([Run the bridge](run-the-bridge.md)).
- You have your phone with the Peachy app, signed in.

## Steps

1. In Beeper Desktop, start a new chat with **@sh-peachybot:beeper.local**,
   the Peachy bridge bot.
2. Send the message **`login`**.
3. The bot asks for your phone number. Send it with the country code, like
   `+1 555 555 0100`.
4. Peachy texts you a code. Send the code to the bot.
5. The bot says it's waiting for approval. On your phone, open **Peachy**
   and tap **Add another device**.
6. Peachy shows three numbers, and the bot then shows one number in Beeper.
   **On your phone, tap the number that matches the bot's.**
7. Wait for the bot to say you're connected. Keep Beeper open until it does.

Your Peachy chats then appear in Beeper's inbox.

## Good to know

- Keep Peachy installed on your phone. You can turn off its notifications
  so you don't get each message twice.
- The bridge shows up in Peachy's **Devices** list as **Beeper** (unless whoever runs it named it something else).
- More answers in the [FAQ](../faq.md).
